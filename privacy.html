Information stored on your device

Your best records, wish text, Daily results and settings (language, sound, theme, difficulty) are stored on this device and are never sent to the developer. The PC (Steam) 1.0 build keeps records only on that computer.

Game Center

On iPhone, Game Center leaderboards stay off until you turn them on. Only after you turn them on and sign in to Apple Game Center does the app submit your Daily score and your free-play best level to Apple's leaderboards. Apple processes your Game Center player identifier (a user ID) and your scores (gameplay content). The purpose is app functionality only, which is showing those leaderboards. Daily reminders

On iPhone you can turn on a daily reminder. It is scheduled only on this device, and nothing is sent anywhere. It stays off until you turn it on, and you can turn it off in Settings. If you have already played that day, that day's reminder is not scheduled.

On iPhone, saving the last moments as a video works the same way. The permission is add-only. The video is written to the Photos app only when you tap save. It is made on this device, and it is not uploaded.

Home Screen widget

The iPhone Home Screen widget shows today's Daily with data that stays on this device. The app and the widget share that data through the app group group.com.uujeong94.mongdol. It is not sent anywhere.

Apple's StoreKit checks on this device whether the Apple account on this device owns Mongdol Pro. You can restore the purchase.

When you share a result, the text and image go through the iOS share sheet to the app you choose, under that app's own policy.
